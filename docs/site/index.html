Nulltrace

An antidetect browser. Your profiles, sessions, cookies and credentials stay in sync across your own machines — through your Google Drive, encrypted on your device.

Download Nulltrace

How Google Drive sync works

Press one button in the app and approve access in your browser. Nulltrace then keeps a folder named nulltrace data in your Google Drive up to date with your encrypted browser profiles — settings, cookies and site sessions, proxies, tags, notes, vault credentials, scripts, and app settings.

  • Your files only. The app uses the drive.file permission, which means it can only read and write the files it created itself. It cannot see, list, or touch any of your other Drive files.
  • Encrypted before upload. Every payload is sealed with AES-256-GCM under a passphrase only you know. Google stores the bytes; it cannot decrypt them.
  • Every machine stays equal. Changes on any machine merge into the shared folder, and every other machine picks them up — including deletions, both ways.
  • No company server sees your data. There is no Nulltrace cloud in the middle. Everything travels between your machines through your own Drive account.

Revoking access

Turn sync off in the app's Cloud settings, or revoke Nulltrace in your Google account security settings at any time. Revoking stops syncing immediately; everything already on your machines stays untouched.
